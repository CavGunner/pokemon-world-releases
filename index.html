The world is the game board

A rainforest shouldn't feel like a parking lot

Four layers of real-world data and a live weather report decide who's out there with you. Go somewhere genuinely different and your Pokédex will show it.

  • Climate
    Köppen-Geiger, 1 km

    Thirty zones, equatorial rainforest through to ice cap. It's bundled in, so the app still knows roughly where it is with no signal.

  • Land cover
    ESA WorldCover

    Tells woodland from farmland from city from marsh — and the park from the street running along the edge of it.

  • Terrain
    Copernicus DEM

    Climb high enough and mountain species start turning up. The slope you're standing on gets drawn into your own map marker, too.

  • Weather
    Open-Meteo, live

    Rain, snow and clear skies all change who's about — and so do dawn, dusk, night, and the season you're in.

Nobody ends up with the same six. You can't go and farm the strongest team, because the strongest team doesn't live near you. You get whoever's around: the one that kept turning up on the walk to work, the one you met the week you were somewhere else, the one you only caught because it was raining. Give it a few months with a Pokémon you'd never have picked off a tier list and it stops being a pick. That's the difference between building a roster and making friends.

What you actually do out there

Deep where it matters

01

Battles that reward knowing things

Proper turn-based fights: the whole type chart, six stats, status conditions, switching, abilities, held items. Know the matchup and you'll take down something ten levels above you. No frantic swiping, no wallet.

02

Every throw is a real roll

The ball you picked, how worn down they are, and how skittish that species is all feed the classic catch-rate maths. The dice are already cast — those three wobbles are just you finding out.

03

Multiplayer means actually meeting

Trades and battles only happen when the other trainer is genuinely near you. Turning up somewhere together is the whole point, not an obstacle in the way of it.

04

Walking is the verb

Ground covered and places you haven't been are what move you forward. There's no AR camera mode — it eats your battery and adds nothing to a good walk.

05

A Pokédex worth opening

Every species in the game, with its full movepool, its evolution line, and a "where found" tab that genuinely tells you which biomes, which levels, and what time of day to go looking.

06

Nothing is for sale

No purchases, no ads, nobody's analytics riding along. Any money in the game is money you earned in the game. It's a fan project made for the love of it, and that's how it stays.

Android

Getting it on your phone

It isn't on the Play Store, so it comes as an APK you install yourself. Four steps, and only the once.

  1. Grab the APK

    Easiest straight from the phone — tap the button below. Otherwise grab it on a computer and send the file across.

  2. Tell Android it's fine

    It'll ask whether your browser is allowed to install apps. Say yes. You only have to do this once.

  3. Head outside

    Say yes to location when it asks — finding out who's around you is the entire game.

  4. Updates sort themselves out

    It checks for a newer build each time you open it and offers to fetch it. New one goes straight over the top: nothing uninstalled, nothing lost.
